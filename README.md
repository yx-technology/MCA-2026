Task 2:  
For the majority of my life, learning how to play an instrument (eg. piano) comes at a cost. Piano classes, piano scores, buying a piano etc. On top of that, most people around me are motivated to play the piano only because its a great talent that parents want their child to aquire and also a status symbol based of one's upbringing. With technology, now people can learn to play a piece without buying the scores. These are made free online. If one has exceptional talent, they can even arrange a piece by listening to the original song and create a piano version. Of course, this could potentially lead to less of a preference for live music and musicians might give up playing the instrument at all since technology can mimick the sounds of a piano. Music is meant to be appreciated and I think technology has helped bring artistes closer to their audiences regardless of where they are. It has also opened up access to the high barrier of entry to learning the piano.

What aspects of MuseScore were intuitive and worked well? What aspects were challenging?

I chose to transcribe the song from scratch into Musescore. When searching for the score online, I found out that there was already a Musescore file with the transcribed song in a Piano version. At 0.99 pounds, it sit behind a paywall. However, I can view the score and even play it, mostly interactive with limited downloading capacity. By transcribing my own, I believe access to it increases dramatically as it can be distributed more easily. When transcribing the music into Musescore, having gone through the initial bumble of the group project yesterday, I was able to recall the details better as I had already made mistakes. However, I think Musescore included alot more details that I would not consider when transcribing it by hand (eg. including hold for the individual notes than for the whole bar). It was also quite slow to transcribe each individual note, although when a bar repeats, my habit of copy and pasting items on my laptop came into good use as it worked out on the first try. It has also been awhile since my music theory lessons and I believe that should someone not have the muscial background or know how to read a score, they might struggle with knowing what to find when transcribing compared to just drawing it out on the score sheet by hand.
