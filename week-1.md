Task 2:  
For the majority of my life, learning how to play an instrument (eg. piano) comes at a cost. Piano classes, piano scores, buying a piano etc. On top of that, most people around me are motivated to play the piano only because its a great talent that parents want their child to aquire and also a status symbol based of one's upbringing. With technology, now people can learn to play a piece without buying the scores. These are made free online. If one has exceptional talent, they can even arrange a piece by listening to the original song and create a piano version. Of course, this could potentially lead to less of a preference for live music and musicians might give up playing the instrument at all since technology can mimick the sounds of a piano. Music is meant to be appreciated and I think technology has helped bring artistes closer to their audiences regardless of where they are. It has also opened up access to the high barrier of entry to learning the piano.
